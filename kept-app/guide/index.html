Kept Setup guide · Mac

Make your first photo book on a Mac

About 15 minutes the first time. Your photos stay on your Mac the whole time, and nothing is uploaded.

1A Mac 2Google Chrome (or Edge) 3An album in the Photos app
  1. 01

    Get Chrome and open Kept in it

    Kept needs Google Chrome or Edge. Safari cannot read and save folders, so it will not work. Download Chrome from google.com/chrome, then open the Kept link you were sent in Chrome.

  2. 02

    Export your album from Photos as JPEG

    In the Photos app, open the album and press ⌘A to select every photo. Choose File ▸ Export ▸ Export Photos…. Set Photo Kind to JPEG, Size to Full Size, and tick Location Information so Kept can group photos by place. Click Export and save them into a new folder, for example Goa photos. Menu names vary a little between macOS versions.

  3. 03

    Make an empty folder for the book

    In Finder, make a new folder, for example Goa book. Keep it separate from the photos folder. Kept saves your finished book here.

  4. 04

    Start the book

    In Kept, click Make a book from photos and choose your photos folder. Chrome asks for permission, so click View files. Kept reads your photos, which takes a minute or two for 200. Leave the tab open.

  5. 05

    Check the chapters, then save

    Kept groups photos by day and place. Hover over a photo and click Split here to start a new chapter, or click Merge with previous chapter. Then click Next: choose a folder for the book, pick your empty book folder, and click Save changes when Chrome asks.

  6. 06

    Wait, then flip through it

    Kept makes copies and pages, which takes a few minutes. Keep the Mac awake and the tab open. Then click View the book. Use ← → to turn pages, S for the page strip, M to mute and Esc to close. Click Edit on a chapter to swap layouts, reorder or delete photos, or add one music file, then click Save.

  7. 07

    Coming back later

    Open Kept, click Open a saved book and choose the book folder. Chrome asks permission every time you do this, which is normal. Click Allow. Back the book folder up like any other folder.

Words Kept uses

Photos folder
The folder of JPEG photos that Kept reads. Your originals are never changed.
Book folder
The empty folder where Kept saves your book.
Chapter
A set of photos from the same day or place. Each has its own folder inside the book folder.
Page
One screen showing two to four photos, never cropped.

If something goes wrong

  • Nothing happens when I click. You are probably in Safari. Open Kept in Chrome.
  • Kept says photos were skipped. They are HEIC files. Export again as JPEG (step 02).
  • The import stopped, or the tab closed. Start again and choose a new, empty book folder.
  • A page looks odd. Take a screenshot and send it over, so it can be fixed.
