From a folder of photos to a finished book

01

Choose your photos folder

Pick the folder your trip photos are in, 200 or more. Kept makes 1080p copies for the book and remembers the date and place from each photo. Your originals are never touched.

02

Check the chapters

A chapter is a set of photos from the same day or place. Kept groups them for you. Split a chapter at any photo, or merge two, before any pages exist.

03

Tidy the pages

Kept lays out each page for you. Swap a layout, reorder photos or whole pages, move a photo to another page, or delete it.

04

Flip through it

Open the book fullscreen on white pages with no text in the way. Open the thumbnail strip to jump to any page.

The thumbnail strip shows one thumbnail per page. The first page of each chapter, outlined here, is its cover.

Privacy

Your photos never leave your device.

Kept is a static web page with no server behind it. It reads photos from a folder you choose and writes the book to a folder you choose. There is no account and no upload, because there is nowhere to upload to.

Open your browser's network tab while you work and see for yourself that no photo is sent anywhere.

Your book is a folder you own

Each chapter is a plain folder holding its photos, page thumbnails, music and a small manifest file. Move a chapter by moving its folder. Back it up the way you back up anything else.

If the app ever disappears, your photos are still right there as ordinary image files.
